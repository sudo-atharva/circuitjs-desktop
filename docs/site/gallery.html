Everyday circuits

Small, working circuits you meet in real projects. The Simulated line is what the simulation measured. Open in browser runs the circuit live; .csim downloads it for the desktop app (also in the app under Circuits → Everyday Circuits). Each card has How to use notes; the guide covers the basics (hover for values, double-click to edit, double-click a graph to measure).

Based on CircuitJS1 by Paul Falstad and Iain Sharp (GPL-2.0-or-later).
