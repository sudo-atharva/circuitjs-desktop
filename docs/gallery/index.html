Everyday circuits

Small, working circuits you meet in real projects. Each one was simulated in CircuitJS Desktop; the result line is what the simulation measured. Open in browser runs it live here (click switches, drag parts, double-click a graph to zoom and measure slope); .csim downloads it for the desktop app.

Basics: hover any part to see its voltage and current (bottom right). Double-click a part to change its value. Click switches and H/L inputs to toggle them. Right-click a part → View in Scope for a graph; double-click a graph to open the big Waveform Viewer (click = cursor A, right-click = cursor B, slope and rise time in the readout). Reset starts the simulation again from zero.

Based on CircuitJS1 by Paul Falstad and Iain Sharp (GPL-2.0-or-later).
